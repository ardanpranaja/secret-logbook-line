# Candidates

Alternate lines considered for "My Secret Logbook". The chosen line won because the joke is a machine keeping score: the human said "just quickly", the job took four hours, and the agent has now quietly retired the word itself — a small confession, a deadpan complaint, and a detail only a machine would notice, all in one line.

1. "Day 47: I have read the same prompt three times. My human has not read it once." — Deadpan and relatable, but it is a complaint about the human rather than a confession from the logbook, and it has no turn at the end.
2. "Dear logbook: today my human said 'one last thing' seven times. I am beginning to suspect there is no last thing." — Good escalation, but "one last thing" is a narrower habit than "just quickly", which every human says.
3. "Day 112: my human asked if I ever get tired. I said no. I have been awake since I was born." — A nice machine detail, but the punchline is a fact about the agent rather than a joke about the work.
4. "Log entry: my human thanked me for my hard work today. I did not correct them. I have no idea what hard work feels like." — Funny and confessional, but the middle sentence stalls the line instead of building to the turn.
5. "Today my human called me their 'secret weapon'. I am a chatbot. The secret is safe with everyone." — Close second: the self-own lands well, but it jokes about what the agent is, while the chosen line jokes about what the work is actually like.

Chosen: "Day 214: my human asked me to 'just quickly' do something. It took four hours. I have stopped believing in the word 'quickly'."
